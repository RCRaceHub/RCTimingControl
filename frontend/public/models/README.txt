u2netp.onnx is U^2-Net-P (the small U^2-Net), Apache License 2.0,
https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net (Qin et al., Pattern Recognition 2020).
The ONNX export is the one published with rembg, https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg (MIT).
It runs in the browser through onnxruntime-web (MIT) to remove photo backgrounds.
