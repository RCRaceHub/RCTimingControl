# Points scale references

Where each championship points scale, TQ point and drop rule in RCTC comes from. A value goes into a preset or a
built-in scoring system only when it is quoted here from a published document. Nothing is added "from memory", and a
change to a built-in scale needs a new reference below first.

Last checked: 2026-11-24.

## BRCA 1/10 off-road (built in)

Source: **2026 BRCA 10th Electric Off Road Rules, "Operating Rules" 2026 V1** (the BRCA 1/10th Off-Road section rules,
15 pages, BRCA website, 1/10 off-road documents).

| Rule | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Points per place | A final winner 130, second 129 "and so on down to 130th place and beyond who will receive 1 point" | 18.2 |
| TQ point | "At National and Regional Sanctioned events the driver who achieves TQ will be awarded one extra point" | 18.2 |
| Rounds counted | National Championship: six events per class, the best four scores count | 5.2 |
| Tied positions in a final | Equal position and points, the next place receiving 2 less | 18.4 |
| Championship ties | The highest scores that counted decide, then the next highest, and so on; otherwise equal | 18.6 |
| A final legs | Three legs, 1 point for a leg win down to 10 for tenth, best two count, lowest wins | 18.3 |
| Qualifying (Round by Round) | 0 points for the best, 2 for second, 3 for third and so on; best 2 rounds count | 15.25 |

The built-in "BRCA 1/10 off-road" championship system and the BRCA preset in the points scale editor use the 130 to 1
scale, a TQ bonus of 1, and best 4 of 6 (migration V63). A Regional series may differ (the East Mids and South West
Regional Series pages say best 3 count and one TQ point; points per place are by final position), so a club running a
regional series sets its own best X.

Also read: BRCA 10th Off Road Mid East Regional Series page (https://www.brca.org/10th-off-road-east-mids-regional-series)
and South West Regionals page (https://www.brca.org/events/10th-off-road-south-west-regionals).

## ROAR

No ROAR championship per-place points table or TQ bonus point has been found, so there is no ROAR preset.

- ROAR National Guidelines, Appendix C (https://www.roarracing.com/downloads/roarnationalsguidelines.pdf, revised
  10/13/2026), 14.9.2.2: AAA main results "0 points for the winner, 2 points for 2nd place in each main on down to 10
  points for the tenth", best two of three mains (14.9.2.3). This scores one National's mains, not a championship.
- ROAR Rule Book (https://www.roarracing.com/downloads/ROAR_Rule_Book.pdf, effective 16 Oct 2026), 3.11.5: best two of
  three qualifying rounds. Section 8 (scoring) could not be read from the web copy.

The old ROAR preset (20, 17, 15, 13, 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 7) had no source and was removed. A club can type its own scale.
To add a ROAR preset, put the quoted rule here first.
