# Points scale references

Where each championship points scale, TQ point and drop rule in RCTC comes from. A value goes into a preset or a
built-in scoring system only when it is quoted here from a published document. Nothing is added "from memory", and a
change to a built-in scale needs a new reference below first.

Last checked: 2026-11-24.

## Confirmed

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BRCA regional championship: TQ point | One bonus point to the TQ driver at each round | BRCA 10th Off Road Mid East Regional Series page (https://www.brca.org/10th-off-road-east-mids-regional-series), "Championship & Regional Championship Points Scoring System"; South West Regionals page (https://www.brca.org/events/10th-off-road-south-west-regionals), "Championship & Points Scoring" |
| BRCA regional championship: rounds counted | Best 3 count (3 of 5 East Mids; South West page says 3 of 4 in its points text but lists five rounds) | Same two pages |
| BRCA regional championship: points per place | "awarded for final position only" (East Mids) and "based on final finishing positions" (South West); no table is printed | Same two pages |
| ROAR national AAA main scoring (within one National, not a championship scale) | 0 points for the winner, 2 for 2nd, on down to 10 for 10th in each main; best 2 of 3 mains | ROAR Appendix C, National Guidelines for National Race Management Team Events (https://www.roarracing.com/downloads/roarnationalsguidelines.pdf, revised 10/13/2026), 14.9.2 |
| ROAR national qualifying | Best 2 of 3 rounds, ROAR Qual-Points System | ROAR Rule Book effective 16 Oct 2026 (https://www.roarracing.com/downloads/ROAR_Rule_Book.pdf), 3.11.5 |

## Not found

No published per-place championship points table was found for either body, and no ROAR TQ bonus point.

- **BRCA:** the 2026 Members Handbook (https://www.brca.org/racer-hub/handbooks/5040-brca-members-handbook-2026-pdf/download) pages read had no table. The "2025 BRCA 1/10th Electric Off-Road Rules" PDF is linked from the BRCA 10th off-road documents page but its download returned an error, so it is unread.
- **ROAR:** the rule book text could only be read as far as section 7.2.2; section 8 (8.7 Scoring) was unreachable.
